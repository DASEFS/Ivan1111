Quality First

Jewelry Quality Control Process

Every piece of jewelry goes through a rigorous 5-step quality inspection process

01

Material Inspection

Raw material quality check: silver purity, alloy composition, zircon grade verification before production.

02

Craftsmanship Check

Polishing and grinding inspection: smooth edges, secure stone setting, precise soldering joints verification.

03

Plating & Anti-ox Test

Electroplating thickness test, anti-oxidation salt spray test, color fastness and nickel-free compliance check.

04

Finished Product QC

100% finished piece inspection: dimension, appearance, stone quality, functionality, and overall aesthetics review.

05

Export Packing Check

Final packaging inspection: anti-tarnish paper, individual poly bags, custom branded boxes, and export carton verification.
